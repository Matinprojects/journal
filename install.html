The journal runs entirely on your own device. No account, no sign-up, and nothing about your trading is ever sent anywhere. Adding it to your home screen makes it full screen and lets it work with no internet at all.

Install it

iPhone & iPad

Must be Safari — other browsers on iOS cannot install
  1. Open the link in Safari
  2. Tap Share — the square with an arrow, at the bottom
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add

Android

Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet
  1. Open the link in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen

Windows & Mac

Chrome or Edge
  1. Open the link
  2. Click the install icon on the right of the address bar
  3. Or menu → Install Trading Journal

Prefer not to install?

Everything still works
  • Just bookmark the link and open it from there
  • Use the same browser every time — your journal is stored in that browser
◈
Where your data lives. Your trades are stored by the browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no server holding it. That also means clearing your browsing data erases it — so once a week open Data → Export CSV. That file is a complete backup and restores everything, tags included.
First ten minutes
  1. Customize — replace the sample strategies with your own, set your session windows to your platform's clock, and mark which mental states are good and bad for you. Everything the analysis says is built on those labels.
  2. Boxes — open a box. Decide how many trades you are committing to and under what rules, then fill in your capital and weekly withdrawal share.
  3. Import — set a start date, then give it your broker statement. In MetaTrader: History → right-click → Report → save as HTML. Duplicates are skipped, so you can drop the same file in every day.
  4. Tag the trades that come in — four keystrokes each.
  5. Before your next trade, press Trade Gate.
